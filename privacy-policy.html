Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MSEDCL (Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Co. Ltd.) and Amnex Infotechnologies Pvt. Ltd. collects, uses, and protects your information through our WhatsApp Business API integration.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information for communication purposes:

  • Phone numbers of authorized MSEDCL employees
  • Employee names and designations
  • Substation and work assignment details

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used solely for:

  • Sending work assignment notifications via WhatsApp
  • Communicating substation status alerts
  • Official MSEDCL operational communication

3. Data Sharing

We do NOT share your personal data with any third party. Data is only used within the MSEDCL and Amnex operational systems.

4. Data Security

All data is stored securely and transmitted via encrypted channels. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

5. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary for operational purposes. Upon request, data will be deleted within 7 business days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.
