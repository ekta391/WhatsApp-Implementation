Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 2026

This page explains how you can request deletion of your personal data collected through the MSEDCL WhatsApp Business API integration.

What Data We Store

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Your name and designation
  • Work assignment records sent to you
  • Message delivery status logs

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send an email to raj5@amnex.com
  2. Subject: "Data Deletion Request - MSEDCL WhatsApp"
  3. Include your full name and registered phone number
  4. We will acknowledge your request within 2 business days
  5. All your data will be permanently deleted within 7 business days
  6. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete

After Deletion

Once your data is deleted:

  • You will no longer receive WhatsApp notifications from MSEDCL
  • Your phone number will be removed from all broadcast lists
  • All message logs associated with your number will be permanently erased

Questions

If you have any questions about data deletion, please contact us directly.
